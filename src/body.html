    Why use {{APP_NAME}}

    • Screenshots for help or proof. A chat, a delivery problem, a bank or app error: send it without your card number, email address or phone number.
    • Photos of people who didn't ask to be online. Children, friends, strangers in the background.
    • Photos of your home, car or street. House numbers, number plates, street signs and parcel labels say where you live.
    • Documents and tickets. ID numbers, boarding-pass barcodes and booking codes can be used by anyone who sees them.
    • Covers that stay covered. A box drawn on top in many editors can be removed again. {{APP_NAME}} replaces the pixels and saves a new picture with no hidden data about you or your phone.

    What {{APP_NAME}} can't find for you

    • Check the whole picture yourself. Detection is a helper, not a guarantee. Cover anything it missed with a box or the brush.
    • Hard to find: faces mostly hidden, turned away or seen from above; very small, blurred or dark faces; handwriting; text at a steep angle, in fancy lettering or under glare (take another photo if you can); reflections; screens and photos inside the picture; tattoos.
    • Names and addresses can't be recognised as such. Type them in Find words and {{APP_NAME}} finds them in every picture.
    • Your original stays as it was. The uncovered picture is still in your gallery and in any cloud backup.
