    What to try

    • Faces. Group photos, a face far in the background, a face in profile, sunglasses, a face on a poster or a screen.
    • Text in photos. Number plates, house numbers, street signs, name badges, a parcel label.
    • Screenshots. A chat, an email, a banking or delivery app, a boarding pass with its barcode.
    • The summary under each picture lists what was found and how long it took, without the file name or anything the picture shows. Copy it to report back.

    Good to know

    • Detection can miss things. That is what this test is for. In the finished app you will always check the whole picture yourself before sharing it.
    • Your pictures stay as they are. {{APP_NAME}} only reads them; nothing is changed or saved.
